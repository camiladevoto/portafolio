CONSULTING · FUTURE17

Mapping emerging
climate-tech solutions.

A team consulting project for Mercy Corps Ventures, examining emerging climate-tech startups across Africa, Latin America and Southeast Asia.

Climate Technology, Startup Research, Evidence Mapping

01The brief

Support the mapping of emerging climate-tech solutions and organise information to help the organisation explore startups aligned with its areas of interest.

02My contribution

I researched emerging climate-tech startups, mapped companies against the project’s classification criteria, and analysed the resulting dataset to identify regional patterns, sector coverage and research gaps.

03Research & approach

The team drew on industry reports, academic sources, government and NGO resources, and startup information platforms. Companies were categorised by geography, sector, funding stage and alignment with Mercy Corps Ventures’ thesis areas and Prepare, Adapt, Recover framework.

04Team deliverables

  • An Airtable database covering 155 startups across 28 countries, as recorded in the final team report.
  • Analysis of regional and sector coverage, with recommendations for further research.
  • A final report and presentation, alongside a team-developed chatbot prototype called Maya Campos.

My role focused on research, mapping and analysis. The database and chatbot were team deliverables; I do not claim sole authorship or chatbot development.

05Findings & limitations

The research highlighted uneven regional coverage and opportunities to expand underrepresented countries and subsectors. The database was a project research sample rather than a complete market census. Recommendations and prototype capabilities are distinct from demonstrated organisational impact.

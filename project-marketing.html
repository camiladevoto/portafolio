The assignment

Create a brand and develop an evidence-informed marketing plan. Illumé Beauty and Radiance Veil were developed as an academic concept, rather than a launched business or an external client engagement.

Brand & product concept

The concept combined an inclusive shade range, skincare-inspired product benefits, a Y2K-influenced identity and proposed glass packaging. These were design and positioning choices within the assignment, not independently tested product claims.

Analysis & strategy

  • PESTLE and SWOT analysis to explore external conditions, risks and opportunities.
  • Porter’s Five Forces and competitor positioning to assess the market context.
  • Customer segmentation, targeting, positioning and value proposition development.
  • A direct-to-consumer approach with proposed Instagram, TikTok and influencer activity.

Commercial planning

The plan included pricing, estimated unit costs, sales scenarios and an expense forecast. All financial figures and audience targets were planning assumptions or forecasts. They were not achieved sales, profits or follower counts.

What this demonstrates

Connecting market research with brand positioning, customer needs, channel choices and commercial assumptions in one structured plan.
